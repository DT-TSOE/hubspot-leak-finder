## Detailed description (long)
HubSpot shows you what's in your pipeline. PipeChamp tells you where it's leaking.

Connect your HubSpot account and PipeChamp analyzes your contacts and deals to produce a single pipeline health score, then shows you exactly where revenue is slipping away — the stages deals stall in, the leads that never get a fast follow-up, the difference between the deals you win and the ones you lose.

Everything is read-only. PipeChamp never writes to, changes, or deletes anything in your CRM. You get answers in about 30 seconds, with no setup and no spreadsheets.

**What you'll see:**
- A pipeline health score with Marketing and Sales sub-grades, tuned to your business.
- Funnel drop-off analysis — where prospects fall out, stage by stage.
- Won vs lost breakdown — what your winning deals have in common.
- Stage aging and at-risk deals — what's stuck and going cold.
- Speed-to-lead — how fast leads get contacted, and what it's costing you.
- Revenue analysis — win rate, sales cycle, and deal value by source.
- Prioritized insights, each with a plain-English fix.
- PipeCoach — an AI assistant that answers questions about your pipeline.

Optionally connect Google Analytics and Search Console to see how marketing traffic feeds your funnel.

Start free — 14 days of full access, no credit card, then keep a free plan for as long as you like.

## Key benefits (the 3-feature block; header + blurb each)
1. **See where revenue is leaking.** One score and a ranked list of what's broken in your funnel — stalled stages, cold leads, slow follow-up — instead of digging through reports.
2. **Know exactly how to fix it.** Every insight comes with a specific, plain-English next step. Ask PipeCoach for more detail on any of them.
3. **Read-only and instant.** Connect in one click, answers in 30 seconds. PipeChamp only ever reads your data — it never changes your CRM.
